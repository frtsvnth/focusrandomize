Character models in this folder ("character-*.glb" + Textures/colormap.png) are the
"Mini Characters" 3D asset pack by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters),
licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No attribution required, credit given voluntarily. Used as-is (GLB, with their own
Idle/Walk animation clips) via three.js's GLTFLoader — unlike the toy car sprites in
../toycars, these are not pre-rendered and are loaded directly by the app at runtime.
